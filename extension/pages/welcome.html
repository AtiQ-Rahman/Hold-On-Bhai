How it works

  1. Pause: open a tracked site and you get a short countdown and one question: "What's the mission?" The wait doubles every time you come back the same day.
  2. Timer: pick 2, 5, 10 or 15 minutes. A small timer sits in the corner with your mission, and a clock goes tick tick while you scroll.
  3. Peep peep: when time's up you hear a beep, get 15 seconds to wrap up, then the tab self-destructs.
  4. Daily budget: Facebook gets 30 minutes a day. When it's gone, it's gone until midnight. No cap.
  5. Stats: see how often you open it, why, and how you felt after. The receipts don't lie.
